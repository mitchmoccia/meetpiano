## Music and audio provenance

All MP-09 figures are original MeetPiano teaching copy and Web Audio synthesis. No third-party recording, no published method piece, and no licensed commercial track.

| Figure | What it is | What it is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft Walk | C4–D4–E4–C4, twice for L21; once for recital option | Not Little Wave (`C–D–E–D–E–D–C`). Not a Faber/RCM tune. |
| Cousin walk | E4–D4–C4–C4, twice | Not a copied climb. |
| Chosen endings | C–D–E plus land-on-C, open-on-G, or turn D–C | No single “correct” melody. G stays in the five-finger place. |
| Purpose jobs | Little Wave (notes), Soft Walk on the clock (rhythm), E–D–C (spot) | Rhythm dump of the same letters must not pass the rhythm lane. |
| Recital shares | Little Wave, Soft Walk, or the stored L22 ending | Not a jury piece. |

Quiet/strong demos use the existing synth at two relative velocities (`0.28` / `0.90`). That is a listening color, not studio-grade scoring.
