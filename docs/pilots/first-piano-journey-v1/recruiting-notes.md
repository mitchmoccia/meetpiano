## Consent and privacy

Say this before any sitting:

- Child data stays **minimal**: first name or a nickname on paper is enough if you need a label; no school ID, no email list for the child.
- No account. No cloud progress. Export is optional and stays on devices the family controls.
- **No recording upload** to MeetPiano. If the family records for themselves, we do not take the file.
- Facilitator notes are usability observations, not a student record.
- They can stop and ask us to discard paper notes from that sitting.

Do not collect photos for marketing. Do not quote a child in public copy from this v1 pack.
