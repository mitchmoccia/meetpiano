# Grown-up sheet — First Piano Journey

A short page for the adult in the room. This is **not** a report card.

## What this is

A first look at little jobs on this computer. Progress stays **on this browser**. There is no account and no grade.

You do not need to play piano. Stay nearby for setup. A short first-sit setup strip may appear on `/learn` or L01 — wake the sound, try a key, skip MIDI if you do not have a keyboard. Cheer the tries. **Enough for today** ends the sit kindly. You may stop any time.

## What to watch

Sit where you can see the keys and the child’s hands.

- Can they find the next yellow job without you taking over?
- High keys and low keys — do they hear a difference?
- Clumps of two black keys and three black keys — can they point?
- If they use Pause, does coming back feel okay?

The app **cannot** see sitting or fingers. If you look at the bench and the hands, that is **your** eyes. You do not have to write a score.

## One thing to try later, off the screen

On a real piano, a toy keyboard, or a paper drawing of keys:

**Point to a clump of two black keys, then a clump of three.** Say “two” and “three.” No app needed. No timer.

If you never get to that, that is fine.

## Words we are not using today

Please skip: grade, pass, fail, level up, “you should be on lesson ___,” teacher-approved, or “this will make you better.”

A helper view lives at `/learn/?view=grown-up`. It lists what **this browser** already stored. It is not a login and not a privacy product.
