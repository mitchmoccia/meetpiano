# Session script — First Piano Journey v1

**Audience:** one child about seven, one grown-up in the room, one facilitator.

**Time:** about 30–40 minutes. Stop sooner if the child is tired or frustrated.

**Path:** setup → First Notes L01 → toward L04 only if it still feels kind → grown-up view → export optional.

Say the lines in **bold** out loud. Plain lines are facilitator notes, not a speech.

Do not grade. Do not say “you should be further.” Do not rush later worlds.

---

## 0. Before they arrive (facilitator only)

- Chrome on a computer, if you have a choice.
- Open `https://meetpiano.app/learn` (or the preview Mitch named). Do not start L01 for them unless they ask.
- Headphones ready but optional.
- A paper keyboard or real piano is nice if they have one. Not required.
- Give the grown-up [`grown-up-prompt.md`](grown-up-prompt.md). You keep [`observation-checklist.md`](observation-checklist.md).
- No camera upload. No cloud recording. If someone wants a phone video for their own memory, that stays on **their** device — we do not collect it.

---

## 1. Welcome (2 minutes)

**“Thanks for trying this with us. This is a first look at First Piano Journey — little jobs on this computer. It is not a teacher and not a grade. We want to see what is confusing, not give you a score.”**

**“You can stop any time. There is no prize for finishing more lessons.”**

If they ask “will this make me better?”: **“We are not testing that today. We just want to see if the buttons and jobs make sense.”**

---

## 2. Setup (about 8 minutes)

**“A grown-up should stay nearby for setup. Progress saves on this browser only. There is no account. If we switch computers later, this progress will not be there unless a grown-up copies the file.”**

1. Grown-up opens Chrome (say why: **“Chrome is the browser we have actually checked. Other browsers might work; we have not written that down yet.”**).
2. Go to `/learn`.
3. Read the yellow line together: saved on this device; not an account; touch is never MIDI verified.
4. Optional MIDI: **“Only if you already have a compatible keyboard. If we skip it, that is fine. On-screen keys are a stand-in.”** Record make / OS / browser if they connect one. Do not fail the sitting if MIDI is absent.
5. Open grown-up view once: `/learn/?view=grown-up`. **“This lists what this browser has already stored. On a fresh visit it should look empty. It is not a login.”** Then return to the journey map.

---

## 3. First Notes — L01 toward L04 (about 18–22 minutes)

**“We will start Meet the keyboard. A yellow job tells you what to do now. You can hear the words if you like. Pause if you want a breath.”**

### L01 · Meet the keyboard

**“See the long row of keys. We will find high and low, and the clumps of two and three black keys.”**

- Let the child lead. Facilitator helps only after a stall.
- Ask the grown-up (quietly) to watch sitting and fingers. **The app cannot see that.**
- Once, ask: **“Want to try Pause?”** Resume. Confirm the try is still waiting.

### L02–L04 only if the child still wants to go

**“We can stop here. Or we can try the next little job if you want.”**

- L02 Find C — doorstep of the two-black-key house.
- L03 Neighbors C–D–E — walking next door.
- L04 First little tune — Little Wave; stop before frustration.

**Do not** open Rhythm Club, Read and play, or later worlds in this sitting unless the child clearly asks and time remains. If they ask, **“Those unlock after earlier jobs on this same device. Today we are staying with First Notes.”**

---

## 4. Grown-up view (about 4 minutes)

**“Grown-up, this card is for you. It shows what this browser stored. It does not grade your child. Posture and fingers are still your eyes.”**

Open `/learn/?view=grown-up`. Look at:

- Honesty line (not a login, not privacy protection)
- Any stored lesson names
- One offline practice idea

**“Nothing here is a teacher mark.”**

---

## 5. Export — optional (about 3 minutes)

**“You do not have to copy anything. If you want a backup on a file you keep, we can export JSON. It stays on computers you control. We will not upload it.”**

Skip if they are done. Do not pressure.

---

## 6. Close (about 2 minutes)

**“That is the sitting. Thank you. We will not send this to a class or put your names on a website.”**

Ask, without rushing:

- **“Was anything confusing?”**
- **“Would you want to try again later on this same computer?”**

Do not offer a discount, a limited-time deal, or “other kids already finished.” Do not ask the child to buy anything.
